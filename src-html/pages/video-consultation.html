How it works remotely

Six steps, from your own home

01

Book your call

Choose a time that works for you, anywhere in the world, and we’ll send a video link.

02

Assessment by video

We talk through your hair history, current thinning and goals, and view your hair over the call.

03

Piece & base recommended

Based on what we see and discuss, we recommend the right topper, wiglet or wig, and base type.

04

Guided self-measurement

We walk you through a simple self-measurement process step by step, so we can build your piece accurately.

05

Colour matching

You send photos in natural light and, where needed, a small hair sample, so we can hand-blend your colour dye free.

06

Delivery & aftercare

Your finished piece is shipped to you, with a follow-up video call to guide fitting, styling and care.
