How pricing works

Every piece is bespoke, these are our guide prices

Every piece is priced by what goes into it: the base type, since silk costs more than mono because of the hand finished parting, the length and weight of Russian hair, and the construction, because a fully hand tied wig takes many times the hours of a topper. Prices shown are for the piece made to measure, colour blended without dye and fitted. A 50% deposit at time of order confirms your piece and the balance is settled on completion, 8 to 12 weeks later. Your exact price is confirmed at consultation.
