Hair loss and thinning

Rediscover your confidence

Made to measure toppers and hand tied medical wigs, real, non surgical solutions to hair loss and thinning.

Hair loss can have a profound impact on a woman's emotional wellbeing. What begins as subtle thinning at the crown or parting can bring feelings of vulnerability, embarrassment and even grief. Everyday moments, like looking in the mirror, become distressing as confidence quietly erodes. It is never simply a cosmetic concern; it is a deeply personal experience.

The causes vary as much as the women it affects. Gradual loss is genetic and progresses slowly, while hormonal shifts from pregnancy, menopause or PCOS can spark diffuse shedding. Stress can send healthy hairs into a resting phase, autoimmune conditions cause patchy loss, and chemotherapy can take hair almost overnight, a different journey altogether.

Whatever the cause, there are real, non surgical answers. For thinning in a specific area, a custom hair topper restores density exactly where it is needed. For significant or total loss, a hand tied medical wig gives a complete, comfortable head of hair that looks and moves like your own. Both are made to measure from Russian hair and colour matched without dye.

You will not be pushed towards a solution before it is right. Start with a free consultation, in salon or by video, and we will help you understand what is happening and which piece fits, gently and without pressure.

Russian hair sourced at source, colour blended without dye, and the only company in the UK offering all 3 custom topper types.

Most wigs and toppers on the market are mass produced from processed, silicone coated hair. Colour is matched without a drop of dye. We select several Russian ponytails, draw individual strands from each and recombine them into multi tonal hair that mirrors your own depth, texture and highlights. It is the single thing that most makes a piece disappear into your hair.

Bases are chosen for how you live: breathable mono for everyday comfort, soft silk where the most realistic scalp is needed, or a hybrid combining both. And you are involved at every stage: from selecting the hair, to choosing the base, to the final fit, in our London and Manchester salons or by video from anywhere in the world, with delivery ready to wear and aftercare guidance included. Not an off the shelf product; a piece created around you.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are hair toppers and how are they different from wigs?+

Hair toppers are lightweight pieces that cover a specific area of thinning, the crown, parting or hairline, rather than the whole head like a wig. They clip into your own hair to restore density exactly where it has been lost, which makes them lighter, cooler and more versatile than a full wig. Choose a topper when you still have enough of your own hair to blend with; choose a wig for more advanced or all over loss.

Mono, silk or hybrid, which topper base is right for me?+

A mono base is breathable and gives the most natural scalp appearance at the parting; a silk base adds softness and the most realistic scalp for crown coverage; a hybrid combines both for wider coverage. The right choice depends on where your thinning is, how much coverage you need and how the piece will sit. We recommend the base for you at your consultation, there is no single right answer.

How does dye free colour matching work?+

Rather than dyeing hair to a swatch, we select several Russian ponytails and draw individual strands from each, then recombine them into multi tonal hair that mirrors your own depth and highlights. The result is a flawless, dye free match, the single thing that most makes a bespoke piece disappear into your own hair. For video clients we match against our Russian hair library from your photos and video.

How long does it take to make a bespoke topper or wig?+

8 to 12 weeks. That covers the full craft: selecting the hair, hand-blending the colour, constructing the base to your measurements, hand-tying the piece and a final inspection before fitting. It is not a wait for stock, it is the time it takes to build a piece this personal by hand. A 50% deposit at time of order confirms your place; the balance is settled on completion.

Can I order without visiting London or Manchester?+

Yes. Every piece is made to measure from a consultation, and the consultation can happen by video from anywhere in the UK or worldwide. We guide you through the handful of measurements we need on the call, colour-match from photos and video against our Russian hair library, and confirm everything with you before your piece goes into production. Remote clients receive the finished piece ready to wear.

Will people be able to tell I’m wearing a topper or wig?+

No. From the Russian hair to the hand blended multi tonal colour and the made to measure base, every detail is designed to mirror natural hair growth. Fitted correctly, a topper blends into your own hair with a realistic parting, and a hand tied wig sits with a natural scalp and movement, both are undetectable, even close up, and remain comfortable for everyday wear.
