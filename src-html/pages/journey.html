The full process

From first enquiry to final fitting

01

Enquiry

You reach out through our contact form, phone or WhatsApp to tell us a little about your hair and what you’re looking for.

02

Consultation booked

We arrange a free consultation at a time that suits you, in London, Manchester, or by video, wherever you are.

03

Assessment

We examine your hair and scalp, discuss the cause and pattern of your thinning, and talk through which type of piece suits you.

04

Piece & base selected

Together we choose the right piece, topper, wiglet or wig, and the right base for your lifestyle and comfort.

05

Measurements taken

We take precise measurements in salon, or guide you through self-measurement by video, to ensure a made to measure fit.

06

Colour matched

We hand-blend Russian hair from multiple ponytails, dye free, to match your natural colour and tonal variation exactly.

07

Handcrafted

Your piece is handcrafted by our specialist team, typically taking 8 to 12 weeks depending on the piece.

08

Fitting & styling

At your fitting we check the fit, trim and style the piece to suit you, and show you how to attach and care for it.

09

Ongoing support

We’re here for adjustments, maintenance and advice long after your first fitting, your journey doesn’t end there.
