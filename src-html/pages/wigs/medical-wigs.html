A wig built entirely around you

For total or near total hair loss, made completely bespoke

Made to measure, finest Russian hair, lightweight and breathable, a discreet, secure fit for a sensitive scalp.

Where thinning has progressed to total or near total hair loss, whether from alopecia, chemotherapy or another medical cause, a bespoke wig offers full coverage, built entirely to your own head shape, hairline and colouring. Nothing is taken from a shelf; every custom wig begins with you.

Every medical wig is hand tied strand by strand onto a made to measure base, using Russian human hair colour matched dye free, strands drawn from several ponytails and recombined to mirror your own depth and highlights, so it moves, falls and catches the light exactly like natural hair, with a realistic scalp and parting even close up.

The full lace base is fine, lightweight and breathable, taken from a complete measurement of your head, especially important for a sensitive scalp during or after medical treatment. A 50% deposit at time of order confirms your wig; handcrafting takes 8 to 12 weeks and the balance is settled on completion.

A bespoke medical wig, explained

A comfortable, full lace base, worn all day

Our medical wigs are built on a fine, breathable full lace cap, fitted to your exact head measurements rather than a standard size. The construction is lightweight enough for all day wear and adjustable for comfort as hair or sensitivity changes over time, a genuinely secure, discreet fit for a sensitive scalp.

Because the cap is made to measure, the hairline sits where your hairline sits and the parting reads as skin, the details that make a hand tied wig undetectable in daily life.

From consultation to final fitting, considered with you

We take time at your consultation to understand your hair history and your preferred colour, length and style, whether that means matching how your hair looked before, or choosing something entirely new. Once confirmed, your wig is handcrafted and fitted with a final styling session, ready to wear with confidence from day one.

For clients who cannot travel, the whole journey works by video: measurements guided on the call, colour matched from photos and video against our Russian hair library, and the finished wig shipped ready to wear, with a trim by your own stylist if you wish.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is a bespoke wig suitable for chemotherapy-related hair loss?+

Yes. Many of our medical wig clients are undergoing or have completed chemotherapy. We fit around a sensitive scalp and can prepare the wig in advance based on your current hair, if you wish to match it before loss occurs.

How is a medical wig different from a shop-bought wig?+

A bespoke wig is built entirely to your own head measurements and hand tied from Russian hair, rather than mass produced on a standard cap. This gives a far more secure, comfortable and natural result.

How long does a bespoke medical wig take to make?+

Once confirmed, a bespoke wig is hand tied strand by strand and typically ready within 8 to 12 weeks, followed by a final fitting and styling session.

Can the wig be adjusted if my head sensitivity changes?+

Yes, the base includes adjustable elements so fit can be refined over time as needed, and we’re on hand for aftercare adjustments.
