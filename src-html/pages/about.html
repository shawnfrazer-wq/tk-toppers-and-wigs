About Tatiana Karelina

Our founder

A specialist in the world’s finest hair

Tatiana Karelina founded her eponymous studio to give women experiencing hair loss and thinning access to the same quality of hair, craft and care found nowhere else in the UK. Over 18 years, she has built direct relationships with Russian hair sources, giving her studio exclusive access to hair prized for its strength, softness and natural lustre.

What began as a specialism in sourcing exceptional Russian hair has grown into a full bespoke service, toppers, wiglets and hand tied wigs, each one crafted individually for the client wearing it.

Hair, after health

We understand what hair loss means beyond appearance

Hair loss is rarely just about hair. It can follow illness, treatment, hormonal change or simply time, and it almost always affects how a woman feels about herself. We approach every consultation with that understanding first, and craft second: listening before recommending, and never rushing a decision that matters this much.
