Make an Enquiry

Before You Order, Every topper and wig is made to measure, so your piece begins with a free consultation in London, Manchester or by video. A 50% deposit at time of order confirms your piece and the balance is settled on completion; handcrafting takes 8 to 12 weeks. Because each piece is bespoke, made to your measurements and colour matched dye free to your own hair, toppers, wiglets and medical wigs are made individually to order. Remote clients receive their piece ready to wear, with a final trim by their own stylist if desired.
