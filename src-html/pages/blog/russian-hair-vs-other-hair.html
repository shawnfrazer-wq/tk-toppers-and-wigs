Russian Hair

Why Russian Hair? What Makes It Different From Chinese and Indian Hair

By Tatiana Karelina, hair topper and wig specialist · Published 28 May 2026

Russian hair differs from Chinese and Indian hair in structure and in what must be done to it before sale. Chinese hair is naturally thick and straight, so to suit Western pieces it is stripped, dyed and silicone coated, processing that leaves it heavy, glassy and short lived. Indian hair is finer but usually dark, so achieving European shades demands aggressive lightening with the same cost to condition. Russian hair is naturally fine, soft and often light, the closest match on earth to European hair texture, so it needs no stripping and no dye. It blends invisibly and lasts for years. At Tatiana Karelina Toppers and Wigs we source Russian hair at source, through a network of trusted collectors built over 18 years, before it reaches brokers who blend it with cheaper Asian hair, and every topper and wig we make is built from it.

The processing problem

Most of the world's hairpiece hair starts as something very different from the finished product. To turn thick, dark hair into a light European shade, factories strip the cuticle in acid, dye the fibre and seal it in silicone. In the packet it gleams; within weeks the coating washes away and the damaged fibre beneath tangles and dulls. No care routine can restore a cuticle that was dissolved.

Why Russian hair skips all of it

Russian hair arrives already fine, already soft, often already light. Nothing needs stripping, so the cuticle stays intact, and colour work happens by blending real shades together rather than dyeing, drawing strands from several ponytails to build your exact colour with its natural depth. Intact hair behaves like hair: it moves, takes heat and lasts years.

Sourced at source

The phrase matters because the supply chain is where quality is lost. By the time hair reaches brokers it is routinely blended, Russian mixed with cheaper processed Asian hair and sold under the same name. Our hair comes through collectors we have worked with for over 18 years, before that blending can happen, so what arrives in the salon is the raw material itself.

Is all hair sold as Russian actually Russian?+

No, and that is the industry's open secret. Blended hair is routinely sold as Russian. Provenance, sourcing at source through known collectors, is the only reliable guarantee.

Does Russian hair cost more?+

Yes, it is the most prized hair in the world and supply is limited. It is also the only hair that blends invisibly with European hair without processing, which for a daily worn piece is the entire point.
