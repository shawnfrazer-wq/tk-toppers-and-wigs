Classic, Frontal or Halo: Which Hair Topper Do I Need?

By Tatiana Karelina, hair topper and wig specialist · Published 26 May 2026

The right topper follows your pattern of thinning. Choose a classic topper for general thinning across the crown and parting: it covers a 5 by 6 inch area and clips into your own hair. Choose a frontal topper for recession at the front hairline and temples, including frontal fibrosing alopecia: its hand ventilated lace edge rebuilds a natural line that withstands close inspection. Choose a halo topper for more advanced or diffuse thinning where there is not enough strong hair to support clips: it holds with a gentle adjustable band instead. Tatiana Karelina Toppers and Wigs is the only company in the UK offering all 3 custom types, each made from Russian hair, colour matched without dye, and handcrafted to your measurements over 8 to 12 weeks. If you are unsure, a free consultation in London, Manchester or by video settles it in under an hour.

Start with where the thinning shows

Stand in daylight and look at where coverage is thinnest. Mostly the parting and crown points to a classic. Mostly the hairline and temples points to a frontal. Spread across a wider area, or hair too fragile to grip clips comfortably, points to the halo. Most women know the answer instinctively; the consultation confirms it and rules out the cases where a wig would honestly serve better.

How the three differ in wear

The classic and frontal attach with secure clips you manage yourself in moments. The frontal adds its lace edge at the front, trimmed to your own hairline shape at fitting. The halo replaces clips entirely with an adjustable band, which spreads hold across the head and puts no strain on thinning hair. All 3 are made from the same Russian hair with the same dye free colour blending, so the finish is identical; only the engineering differs.
