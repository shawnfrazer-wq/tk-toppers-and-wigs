Alopecia Areata and Alopecia Universalis: Your Wig and Topper Options

By Tatiana Karelina, hair topper and wig specialist · Published 30 June 2026

The right piece for alopecia depends on the extent of loss. Alopecia areata causes patchy bald spots, and while patches are limited a made to measure hair topper can cover them, blending with the hair around each area. When loss becomes extensive, or with alopecia totalis and universalis where scalp hair is lost entirely, a bespoke medical wig is the answer: a complete head of hair, hand tied strand by strand onto a breathable full lace cap fitted to your exact head measurements. At Tatiana Karelina Toppers and Wigs every wig is made from Russian hair with a dye free colour match, with guide prices from £2,600 to £3,725 and an 8 to 12 week handcrafting time. Consultations are free and compassionate, in London, Manchester or by video from anywhere in the world.

Topper or wig: where is the line?

While your own hair can still anchor and blend with a piece, a topper is the lighter option: a classic for patches at the crown and parting, a frontal for the hairline, and the clip free halo when hair is too fragile or sparse for clips. Once loss is extensive or total, the honest answer is a wig, and a bespoke one behaves nothing like the wigs most people imagine.

What makes a bespoke alopecia wig different?

A cap fitted to your exact measurements rather than a standard size, with a choice of lace front, monofilament or hand knotted constructions that mimic a natural scalp and let air flow. Soft linings and adjustable straps keep it gentle and secure on a bare scalp. The hair is Russian, hand blended without dye, and the individual knot technique lets every strand move freely, so the wig reads as growth, not as a wig.

Living with it day to day

A well made wig is worn all day in comfort, removed to sleep, and cared for like natural hair with sulphate free products. We provide aftercare guidance and ongoing support, adjustments as your needs evolve, care advice whenever you want it, because alopecia is a journey and the piece has to keep up with it.

Which is right for patchy alopecia areata?+

While patches are limited and surrounded by healthy hair, a topper covers them discreetly. If patches multiply or merge, the halo topper or a wig gives broader, gentler coverage. A consultation settles it honestly.

8 to 12 weeks from confirmed design to final fitting, covering hair selection, dye free colour blending, cap construction to your measurements and quality checks.
