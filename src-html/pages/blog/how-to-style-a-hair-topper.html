How to Style a Hair Topper So It Looks Natural

By Tatiana Karelina, hair topper and wig specialist · Published 6 June 2026

A hair topper looks natural when 3 things line up: placement, blend and colour. Position the piece so its parting sits where your natural parting falls, clip it securely front to back, then blend your own hair through and over the edges so no line shows. Colour does the heaviest lifting, which is why every Tatiana Karelina topper is colour matched without dye, strands drawn from several Russian ponytails and blended by hand to mirror your own depth and highlights. Because our toppers are made from unprocessed Russian hair, they can be styled like your own: heat, waves, updos and ponytails are all possible, within sensible limits. Style with low heat, use sulphate free products, and store the piece on a stand. Cared for this way, a bespoke topper keeps its softness and colour for 2 years or longer.

Placement is half the job

Set the front of the topper a finger or two behind your natural hairline unless it is a frontal piece designed to rebuild the line itself, and align its parting with yours. Clip front first, then back, so the base sits flat. A made to measure piece makes this almost automatic, because the base was built for your head and the clip positions were decided at your fitting.

Blending and partings

Draw a little of your own hair over the front edge and sides, then smooth topper and hair together. Because the piece is Russian hair, it responds to a brush and heat exactly as yours does, so the two blend into one head of hair. Partings are where cheap pieces fail: a silk or mono base recreates skin at the parting, which is why we match the base to how you wear your hair.

Heat, products and care

Use low heat and always on dry hair, keep oils away from the base, and wash gently with sulphate free shampoo, then air dry on a stand. We walk every client through the exact routine for their piece at fitting, and remote clients receive the same guidance with their delivery.

Can I curl or straighten my topper?+

Yes. Russian hair takes heat styling like your own hair. Keep temperatures moderate, use protection, and let the piece rest on a stand between wears.

Can I wear a ponytail with a topper?+

With a well fitted piece, yes, particularly with a frontal topper whose lace edge withstands hair worn away from the face. Ask at your fitting and we will show you how.
