Female Hair Loss: What Are Your Options in 2026?

By Tatiana Karelina, hair topper and wig specialist · Published 2 June 2026

Why is my hair thinning?

Female hair loss has many causes and they behave differently. Female pattern hair loss is genetic and progresses slowly at the crown and parting. Hormonal shifts from pregnancy, menopause or PCOS can cause diffuse shedding, stress can push healthy hairs into a resting phase, and autoimmune conditions such as alopecia areata cause patchy loss. Chemotherapy can take hair almost overnight. Identifying the cause matters, because it tells you whether loss is likely to be temporary or progressive, so if your loss is sudden or patchy, see your GP or a dermatologist first.

What are the non surgical options?

Once any medical route is underway, the practical question is coverage. A custom hair topper is the answer for partial loss: a lightweight piece, made to your measurements, that blends invisibly with your own hair at the crown, parting or hairline. How do I choose between a topper and a wig?

The honest test is how much of your own hair remains. If you have enough healthy hair to blend with, a topper is lighter, cooler and easier to live with. If loss is significant, total, or your hair is too fragile to support clips, a medical wig is the better answer. Can hair loss be reversed?+

Some types can. Post pregnancy shedding usually resolves within a year, and loss from stress often recovers once life stabilises. Genetic pattern loss is usually progressive. Whatever the cause, a custom topper or wig restores coverage immediately while any medical route takes its course.
