How to Choose Your First Wig for Chemotherapy: A Gentle Guide

By Tatiana Karelina, hair topper and wig specialist · Published 12 June 2026

If chemotherapy is ahead of you, the kindest time to choose a wig is before treatment begins, while your natural colour and style are there to match. A first chemotherapy wig should be judged on 3 things: comfort on a sensitive scalp, a natural look, and support around you. At Tatiana Karelina Toppers and Wigs, medical wigs are hand tied from Russian hair onto breathable caps, lace front, monofilament or hand knotted, with soft linings and adjustable straps that adapt as scalp sensitivity changes through treatment. Colour is matched by hand from several Russian ponytails without dye, so the wig can mirror the hair you have now. A bespoke wig takes 8 to 12 weeks to make, with guide prices from £2,600 to £3,725, and consultations are free, compassionate and unhurried, in London, Manchester or by video.

When should I start looking?

Ideally before hair loss begins. Matching colour, texture and style is simplest from your own hair, and having the wig ready removes one unknown from a season full of them. If treatment has already begun, photographs work well for colour matching, and video consultations mean the whole process can happen from home on the days you feel up to it.

What makes a wig comfortable during treatment?

Scalp sensitivity changes week to week during chemotherapy, so the cap matters more than anything. Breathable constructions let air flow, soft cotton linings sit gently against bare skin, and adjustable straps let the fit adapt as you need. Lightweight construction means the wig is worn all day without awareness of it, which is the entire point.

Will it look like me?

That is the purpose of bespoke. Your cap is made to your head measurements, the hairline sits where yours sits, and Russian hair blended without dye recreates your colour with its natural depth and highlights. Many clients choose to match their pre treatment hair exactly; some choose something new. Both are honoured, and a final fitting with gentle trimming makes the wig entirely yours.

When will I need the wig?+

Chemotherapy induced loss often begins 2 to 4 weeks into treatment. Because a bespoke wig takes 8 to 12 weeks, starting early matters; we prioritise sensitively where timelines are tight, so speak to us as soon as you can.

Can I manage the whole process from home?+

Yes. Measurements are guided on a video call, colour is matched from photos and video against our Russian hair library, and the finished wig arrives ready to wear.
