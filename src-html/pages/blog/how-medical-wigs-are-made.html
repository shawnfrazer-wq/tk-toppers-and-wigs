How Is a Bespoke Medical Wig Made? The Step by Step Process

By Tatiana Karelina, hair topper and wig specialist · Published 19 June 2026

A bespoke medical wig is made in 5 stages over 8 to 12 weeks. First, a consultation and full head measurement, in our London or Manchester salon or guided by video, captures the exact shape the cap will be built to. Second, colour matching by hand: strands drawn from several Russian ponytails and recombined without dye into a blend that mirrors your own depth and highlights. Third, cap construction, lace front, monofilament or hand knotted, with soft linings and adjustable straps for sensitive scalps. Fourth, hand tying: each strand knotted individually so hair lies flat and moves freely, creating the illusion of natural growth. Finally, quality checks and a fitting with gentle trimming, or delivery ready to wear for remote clients. A 50% deposit at time of order confirms the wig; the balance is settled on completion at Tatiana Karelina Toppers and Wigs.

Measurement: the foundation

Every cap begins with a complete measurement of your head, not a standard size. It is why the hairline of a finished wig sits where your hairline sits and why the cap stays secure without gripping. On video consultations we guide the measurements step by step on the call and confirm everything before production begins.

Colour without dye

Rather than dyeing hair to a swatch, we select several Russian ponytails and draw strands from each, recombining them into multi tonal hair. Natural hair is never one colour; it is depth, undertone and highlights together, and only a blend reproduces that. It is the single detail that most makes a wig disappear.

Hand tying and finishing

Thousands of individual knots attach hair to the cap one strand at a time, following growth direction, so the finished wig moves like hair rather than like a curtain. After construction come quality checks, then your final fitting: adjustments, gentle trimming, and guidance on wear and care, or shipping ready to wear with a trim by your own stylist if you prefer.

Why does it take 8 to 12 weeks?+

Because nothing is pre made. Sourcing the right hair, blending your colour, building a cap to your measurements and hand tying every strand are skilled hours that cannot be compressed without losing what makes the wig bespoke.

What if my measurements change during treatment?+

Caps carry adjustable straps precisely for this, and we provide adjustments and ongoing support after fitting as your needs evolve.
