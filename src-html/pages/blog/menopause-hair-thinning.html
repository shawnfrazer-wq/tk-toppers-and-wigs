Why does menopause cause hair thinning?

Oestrogen supports the growth phase of the hair cycle. As it falls through perimenopause and menopause, more follicles rest, fewer grow, and each hair tends to become finer. The result is a gradual loss of density, most visible at the parting and crown, rather than bald patches. Because it is gradual, many women adapt around it for years, flat styles, careful partings, avoiding bright light, before looking for a real answer.
