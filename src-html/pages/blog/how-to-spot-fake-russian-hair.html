Russian Hair

How to Spot a Salon Selling Fake Russian Hair

By Tatiana Karelina, hair topper and wig specialist · Published 3 July 2026

Genuinely Russian hair is scarce, expensive and almost never uniform, so the fakes give themselves away in predictable ways. Be suspicious of low prices: real Russian hair costs what scarcity costs, and a full piece priced like a processed one is a processed one. Be suspicious of perfect uniformity: real ponytails vary naturally in tone and texture, while stripped and dyed hair is eerily identical strand to strand. Be suspicious of high shine: intact cuticle gives a soft lustre, and glassy shine is silicone coating that will wash away. Above all, ask about provenance. At Tatiana Karelina Toppers and Wigs, Russian hair is sourced at source through collectors we have worked with for over 18 years, before brokers blend it with cheaper Asian hair, and we will happily talk through that supply chain at any free consultation, in London, Manchester or by video.

The price test

Scarce raw material cannot be cheap. When a piece advertised as Russian hair costs what mass produced processed hair costs, the description is doing the selling and the hair will do the disappointing. Compare like for like and treat outliers as the warning they are.

The look and feel tests

Run strands between your fingers. Real unprocessed hair feels soft but not slippery, and its shine is quiet rather than glassy. Look along a weft or ponytail: natural hair varies subtly in tone; factory processed hair is uniform because it was dyed in a vat. Wet a strand where possible, silicone coatings turn slick and squeaky in water.

The questions an honest seller can answer

Where does the hair come from, and how directly? Is the colour achieved by dye or by blending? May I see unprocessed ponytails? Honest sellers enjoy these questions. Evasive answers, or colour achieved by dyeing to a swatch, tell you the provenance story ends at a broker's warehouse.

Why does fake Russian hair matter if it looks fine at first?+

Because processing is a debt that comes due. Stripped, dyed and coated hair looks right for weeks, then dulls, tangles and mats. A piece is a daily companion for years; provenance decides which years those are.
