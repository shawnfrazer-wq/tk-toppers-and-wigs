Frontal Fibrosing Alopecia: Non Medical Options That Look Natural

By Tatiana Karelina, hair topper and wig specialist · Published 23 June 2026

Frontal fibrosing alopecia, or FFA, causes a receding band of loss along the front hairline and temples, and medical treatment focuses on slowing progression rather than restoring the lost line. The most natural cosmetic answer is a frontal hair topper: a made to measure piece designed specifically to rebuild the front hairline, with a fine hand ventilated lace edge that sits invisibly against the skin. At Tatiana Karelina Toppers and Wigs, every frontal topper is made from Russian hair, colour matched to your own without dye, with guide prices from £615 to £1,335 and an 8 to 12 week handcrafting time. It is fitted in our London or Manchester salons or arranged by video, and it withstands the tests that matter with FFA: hair worn away from the face, close inspection and direct light.

What makes FFA hard to disguise?

FFA presents at the most visible part of the head. A widening band at the hairline and temples cannot be covered by a centre parting or a fringe forever, and generic hairpieces fail exactly where FFA needs them to succeed, at the edge. Any piece that does not have a genuinely realistic front hairline reads as a hairpiece immediately. We always recommend seeing a dermatologist about FFA itself; our work is the cosmetic restoration alongside it.

How does a frontal topper rebuild the hairline?

The front edge of a frontal topper is hand ventilated onto fine lace, each hair knotted individually so it lies flat and follows natural growth direction. The lace is trimmed and fitted to your own hairline shape at your fitting, so the new line follows your face rather than a standard pattern. Combined with Russian hair and a dye free colour blend, the rebuilt hairline passes at conversation distance and closer.
