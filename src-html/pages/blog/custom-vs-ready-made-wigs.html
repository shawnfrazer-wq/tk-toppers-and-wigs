Ready to Wear vs Custom Made: Which Wig Should You Choose?

By Tatiana Karelina, hair topper and wig specialist · Published 26 June 2026

A ready to wear wig is made to a standard size and stock colour and can be worn the day you buy it. A custom made wig is built to your head measurements, in a colour blended for you, over 8 to 12 weeks. Ready to wear suits short term needs and tight budgets, and its compromises are exactly where wigs are judged: a hairline that sits close but not quite right, a colour near yours but flat, a cap that grips because it cannot fit. A custom wig from Tatiana Karelina Toppers and Wigs is hand tied from Russian hair onto a cap made to your exact measurements, with a dye free colour blend drawn from several ponytails, guide priced £2,600 to £3,725. For hair worn every day, through work, weather and close conversation, made to measure is the difference between wearing a wig and having hair.

What ready to wear does well

Speed and price. If loss is expected to be brief, if a wig is an occasional accessory, or if budget rules, a good ready made piece serves honestly. The best of them, adjusted by a skilled fitter, can look convincing at a distance, and for some seasons of life that is exactly enough.

Where custom earns its keep

Daily wear exposes every compromise. A standard cap on a non standard head grips or slips; a stock colour reads flat in daylight; a machine made hairline shows at close range. Custom answers each: measured fit, hand blended colour with natural depth, and a hand tied front that reads as growth. Comfort compounds too, because a cap built for your head with soft linings is forgotten within minutes of wearing it.

Making the decision

Ask 3 questions. How long will I wear it: months of daily wear favour custom. How close will people be: family, colleagues and cameras favour custom. What does my scalp need: sensitivity during treatment favours a custom cap. A free consultation will answer honestly, including when ready to wear is genuinely the sensible choice.

Can a ready made wig be altered to fit?+

Within limits. Straps adjust circumference, but a standard cap cannot become a measured one, and stock colour cannot become a blend. Alterations narrow the gap; they do not close it.

Is custom worth it for temporary loss?+

Sometimes. For chemotherapy clients wearing a wig daily for a year or more, comfort and realism repay the investment; for shorter journeys we will say honestly if ready to wear serves you better.
