Wiglets

A smaller solution

The smallest of our pieces, for the most targeted needs

A wiglet is our smallest piece, designed to add volume to a very specific, contained area, often a small patch at the crown or along part of the parting. It clips in quickly and is a discreet, low-commitment way to boost density where your own hair needs the smallest amount of extra help.

Wiglets are made from the same Russian hair as our toppers and wigs, and are colour matched dye free for a seamless finish.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between a wiglet and a topper?+

A wiglet is smaller than a topper, designed for a very specific, contained area of thinning rather than a broader zone such as the crown and parting together.

How much does a wiglet cost?+

Wiglets are priced within our topper structure, confirmed once we have assessed your thinning and chosen the right size and base at your consultation.

Is a wiglet suitable for early-stage thinning?+

Yes, it is often the right first step for early, localised thinning, offering a discreet and low-commitment way to add density.
