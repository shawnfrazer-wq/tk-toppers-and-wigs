What are hair toppers?

Coverage exactly where you need it

Lightweight, breathable pieces that restore coverage precisely where it is needed: lighter, cooler and more versatile than a full wig.

Hair toppers, sometimes called wiglets, are lightweight hairpieces designed to cover specific areas of thinning or hair loss on the scalp. Unlike full wigs, they do not cover the entire head: they blend seamlessly with your natural hair, adding volume and coverage exactly where it is needed, which makes them lighter, cooler and far easier to wear daily.

We offer 3 types, classic, frontal and halo, because there is no one size fits all answer to thinning. Each topper is custom made from Russian hair, colour blended without dye to match your natural shade, and designed to give back confidence through discreet, natural coverage.

Toppers are best for women experiencing partial loss: general thinning at the crown or parting, recession at the front hairline or temples, or more advanced thinning where there is not enough hair to support full clips. If your loss is significant or total, a medical wig is usually the better answer, and we will tell you so honestly at your consultation.

Every piece begins the same way: a free, no obligation consultation, in London, Manchester or by video, where we look at your pattern of thinning, colour match by hand and recommend the right piece without pressure. Once confirmed, your topper is handcrafted over 8 to 12 weeks.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are hair toppers?+

Lightweight hairpieces that cover specific areas of thinning or hair loss rather than the whole head. They blend seamlessly with your natural hair, adding volume and coverage exactly where it is needed. We offer classic, frontal and halo toppers, all custom made from Russian hair for a natural result.

When should I choose a topper rather than a wig?+

Choose a topper if you have partial loss or thinning and enough of your own hair to blend with. A classic suits crown and parting thinning, a frontal suits the hairline and temples, and a halo suits more advanced thinning where clips will not hold. For significant or total loss, a medical wig is the better answer.

What makes your toppers different from others on the market?+

Most wigs and toppers are mass produced from processed, silicone coated hair. Ours are custom made from Russian hair and fully tailored to you, and we are the only company in the UK offering all 3 custom topper types, with personalised colour blending, base selection and fit.

Mono, silk or hybrid, which base is right for me?+

Mono is breathable with a natural scalp appearance; silk gives the most realistic parting of all; a hybrid combines both in one piece, silk in the parting for realism and mono elsewhere for lightness. We recommend the right base for your thinning and lifestyle at consultation.

How is the colour of my topper matched?+

Without dye. We select several Russian ponytails and draw strands from each to create a bespoke, multi tonal blend that is sewn into the topper. A flat, single shade never convinces; a hand blended match disappears into your own hair.

Will my topper stay in place?+

Yes. The attachment method and base are chosen for your level of hair and area of coverage, so a properly fitted topper feels secure and comfortable all day while remaining easy to remove yourself.

How long does a custom topper take to make?+

Once your design is confirmed, 8 to 12 weeks. That covers sourcing the hair, blending your colour, building the base to your measurements and final quality checks before fitting.

How do I care for my topper?+

Gently, much like natural hair: suitable sulphate free products, careful brushing, and proper storage on a stand. We guide you on the exact routine for your piece so it stays beautiful for as long as possible.
