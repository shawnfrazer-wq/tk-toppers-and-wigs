The detail, in brief

A Halo Topper, explained

A gentler fit for fragile or thinning hair

Because the Halo relies on an adjustable band rather than multiple clips, it puts far less strain on your own hair and scalp. That makes it particularly suited to clients whose hair is fragile, or whose thinning is too advanced for clips to anchor securely, broad coverage without asking anything of the hair underneath.

The band is adjusted to you at your fitting, so the piece sits comfortably through a full day, secure enough for ordinary activity, but easy to put on and take off yourself at home without help.

Between a topper and a wig, made to measure

The Halo occupies the ground between a targeted topper and a full wig: wider coverage than a Classic or Frontal, while keeping some of your own hair visible underneath, so the result still reads as your hair on your head, lighter and cooler than a full wig ever feels.

If your loss is total or near total, a hand tied medical wig is usually the better answer, and we will tell you so honestly at consultation. Either way, the piece is made individually to order from Russian hair and colour matched dye free to you.

Is a Halo Topper right for you?

The right fit for wider or more advanced thinning

The Halo is the piece we recommend when thinning has moved beyond a single area, when coverage is needed across a wider spread of the scalp, or when your own hair is no longer strong enough to carry clips comfortably, but a full wig is more than you need.

  • Your thinning covers a wider area than clips can comfortably anchor to
  • Your hair is fragile and you want a gentler method of attachment
  • You want a piece you can put on and take off easily each day, without help
  • You want broader coverage while keeping some of your own hair visible underneath

Like every Tatiana Karelina piece, a Halo is made individually to order, Russian hair, hand blended colour and a base built to your measurements, so the coverage is broad but the look is entirely, convincingly yours. A 50% deposit at time of order confirms your piece, handcrafting takes 8 to 12 weeks, and the balance is settled on completion, with fitting in London or Manchester, or entirely by video, worldwide.

Cared for gently, sulphate free products, no heavy oils at the base and a stand for storage, a Halo keeps its softness, colour and secure fit through years of everyday wear.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does a Halo Topper attach without clips?+

It uses an adjustable band that sits comfortably around the head, rather than clipping directly into your own hair. This makes it gentler on fragile hair while still holding securely.

Is a Halo Topper suitable for more advanced hair loss?+

Yes. Its wider coverage and band-based attachment make it a good option when thinning is more advanced or diffuse, or when there isn’t enough healthy hair for clips to grip.

Can I still see some of my own hair underneath?+

Yes, the Halo Topper is designed to work with your remaining hair, blending it with the piece rather than covering it entirely, for a natural result.

How long does a Halo Topper take to make?+

Once confirmed, your Halo Topper is handcrafted from Russian hair and colour matched dye free, typically ready within 8 to 12 weeks.
