Rebuilding the hairline

A soft, undetectable hairline where you need it most

Natural, discreet front hairline coverage, ideal for receding hairlines, temple loss and frontal fibrosing alopecia.

The Frontal Topper is designed specifically for thinning or receding at the front hairline and temples, an area that is difficult to disguise and often the first place hair loss becomes noticeable. It rebuilds a soft, natural hairline using a fine lace edge that sits invisibly against the skin, so the most visible part of your head is also the most convincing.

Like every piece we make, it is crafted from Russian hair and colour matched dye free, strands drawn from several Russian ponytails and recombined into multi tonal hair that mirrors your own depth and highlights, so the transition from piece to your own hair is completely undetectable, even close up and in direct light.

The base, the coverage and the hair length are all made to your measurements, decided at a free consultation in London, Manchester or by video. Your piece is then handcrafted over 8 to 12 weeks, with a 50% deposit at time of order and the balance settled on completion.

The detail, in brief

A Frontal Topper, explained

Fine lace, hand ventilated for a natural hairline

The front edge is hand ventilated onto fine lace, with hair knotted individually so it lies flat and follows a natural growth direction. This is what allows the hairline to be worn away from the face, swept back, in a ponytail, or in direct light, without revealing the piece.

It is careful, slow work: each knot is placed by hand, and the lace is trimmed and fitted to your own hairline shape at your fitting, so the edge follows your face rather than a standard pattern. It is the reason a Frontal Topper passes at the most unforgiving part of the head.

Secure, comfortable and easy to live with

A Frontal Topper attaches with clips and, where extra security helps, a thin bonding strip along the lace edge, both fitted and demonstrated at your consultation so you can put the piece on and take it off confidently at home, without help.

Worn correctly it stays put through an ordinary day, styling and weather included. Care for it much like your own hair, with gentle sulphate free products and a stand for storage, and the Russian hair will keep its softness and lustre for years of regular wear.

Is a Frontal Topper right for you?

The right fit for hairline and temple thinning

The Frontal is the right piece when the front of your hair is what you notice first, a widening parting at the front, a receding line at the temples, or the early stages of frontal fibrosing alopecia, while the rest of your hair still has good coverage. For thinning at the crown, the Classic Topper is usually the better match; we help you decide at your consultation.

  • Your thinning or recession is concentrated at the front hairline or temples
  • You want to wear your hair away from your face with confidence
  • You want a piece that withstands close inspection and direct light
  • You want a natural, undetectable finish at the most visible part of the head

Every Frontal Topper is made individually to order from Russian hair, colour matched dye free to your own shade, so the new hairline is not just covered, it is convincingly, comfortably yours. A 50% deposit at time of order confirms your piece, handcrafting takes 8 to 12 weeks, and remote clients receive their topper ready to wear, wherever they are in the world.

Frequently Asked Questions

What makes a Frontal Topper different from a Classic Topper?+

A Frontal Topper is built specifically to rebuild the front hairline and temples, using a fine hand ventilated lace edge. A Classic Topper is designed for thinning at the crown and parting rather than the hairline itself.

Can I wear my hair swept back or in a ponytail?+

Yes. The hand ventilated lace edge is designed to withstand close inspection and direct light, so you can wear your hair away from your face with confidence.

How is it attached?+

It attaches with clips and, in some cases, a thin bonding strip along the lace edge for extra security, fitted and demonstrated at your consultation.

How long does a Frontal Topper take to make?+

Once confirmed, your Frontal Topper is hand ventilated and colour matched dye free, typically ready within 8 to 12 weeks.
