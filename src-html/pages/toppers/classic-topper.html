The everyday solution

Coverage that blends seamlessly with your own hair

Bespoke coverage for thinning areas, the finest Russian hair, a hand blended colour match and a lightweight, discreet, secure fit.

The Classic Topper is our most versatile piece, designed for general thinning across the crown and parting. It covers a 5″ by 6″ area, clipping securely into your own hair to restore volume and density exactly where you need it most, a denser parting, a fuller crown and the freedom to style your hair without planning around thin areas.

Every Classic Topper is made from Russian hair on a made to measure base. Rather than dyeing to a swatch, we draw individual strands from several Russian ponytails and recombine them into multi tonal hair that mirrors your own depth and highlights, so the join between your hair and the piece disappears, even in direct light, even close up.

Because the base, the coverage area, the clip positions and the hair length are all decided at your consultation and built to your measurements, the finished piece sits flat, blends invisibly at the parting and stays secure through an ordinary day, and with the right care it lasts 2 years or longer.

The detail, in brief

A Classic Topper, explained

Mono or silk, chosen for how you wear it

The Classic Topper is available on a mono base or a silk base. A mono base is breathable and comfortable for everyday wear, with a natural scalp appearance at the parting. A silk base gives the most realistic scalp illusion of all, recreating the appearance of hair growing directly from the skin.

There is no single right answer, the choice depends on where your thinning sits, how much coverage you need and how you will wear the piece day to day. We talk you through both bases at your free consultation and recommend the one that fits your pattern of thinning and your lifestyle.

Made to your measurements, over 8 to 12 weeks

Nothing about a Classic Topper is standard. The coverage area, the clip positions, the hair length and the colour are all decided with you at consultation, then the piece is handcrafted over 8 to 12 weeks, hair selected, colour hand blended, base constructed and every detail checked before your fitting.

A 50% deposit at time of order confirms your piece and the balance is settled on completion. Fittings happen in our London and Manchester salons, or entirely by video, remote clients receive their topper ready to wear, wherever they are in the world.

Is a Classic Topper right for you?

The right fit for moderate, general thinning

The Classic is the topper most of our clients wear day to day. It suits you when your own hair is still healthy around the sides and back, and the change you want is fuller coverage on top. If your thinning is concentrated at the front hairline, a Frontal Topper may fit better; if it has spread wider than clips can comfortably anchor to, the clip free Halo is designed for exactly that, we help you choose at consultation.

  • You have thinning concentrated at the crown or along the parting
  • You still have enough of your own hair around the area to clip into
  • You want a piece that is quick to put on and take off yourself, day to day
  • You want the most natural looking, breathable option for regular wear

Fitted with secure clips that grip your own hair comfortably, a Classic Topper stays firmly in place through daily activity while remaining easy to remove whenever you wish, and you are shown exactly how to fit and remove it yourself at your consultation. Cared for gently, sulphate free products, no heavy oils at the base, a stand for storage, a Classic Topper keeps its softness and colour for 2 years or longer of regular wear.

Frequently Asked Questions

What area does a Classic Topper cover?+

A Classic Topper covers approximately 5 inches by 6 inches, which is enough to address thinning across the crown and parting while leaving your own hair around the edges to blend seamlessly.

How is it attached?+

It attaches with small clips sewn into the base, which grip your own hair securely. It can be fitted and removed yourself at home once you are shown how at your consultation.

Mono base or silk base, which should I choose?+

A mono base is lightweight and breathable for everyday wear. A silk base gives the most realistic scalp illusion. We recommend the right option for you based on your thinning pattern and lifestyle at your consultation.

How long does a Classic Topper take to make?+

Once confirmed, your Classic Topper is handcrafted from Russian hair and colour matched dye free, typically ready within 8 to 12 weeks.
