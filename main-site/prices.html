Frequently Asked Questions

Why do women choose Tatiana Karelina for hair extensions, toppers and wigs?

Every piece we fit is made from genuine Russian hair, colour blended without dye and matched to your natural texture, then fitted by specialists who have refined their craft over nearly two decades in London and Manchester. Prices are published in full so you always know where you stand, your exact quotation is confirmed at consultation before you commit, and the hair itself can be reused for years, which makes the investment go further than cheaper alternatives.

Are fittings included in the price of hair extensions?

For micro ring hair extensions and micro bond hair extensions, the price includes both the hair and the cost of fitting. For tape hair extensions and weft hair extensions, fittings are not included in the listed price and are charged separately at £120 per hour.

How often will I need maintenance and what does it cost?

Maintenance depends on the method. Tape hair extensions and weft hair extensions require appointments every 6 to 8 weeks. Micro ring hair extensions and micro bond hair extensions typically require maintenance every 3 months. Maintenance for micro rings and bonds is charged at half the original fitting cost, while tapes and wefts are charged at £120 per hour. At each visit, the extensions are adjusted, retipped, and refreshed, leaving you with what feels like a brand new set.

What do hair toppers and medical wigs cost?

Frontal toppers start from £615, Classic toppers from £1,050 and Halo toppers from £1,100, rising with length and base. Fully hand tied medical wigs run from £2,600 at 14 inches to £3,725 at 24 inches. Every piece is made to measure from Russian hair over 8 to 12 weeks, with your exact quotation confirmed at consultation.

Do you offer same day consultations and fittings?

Yes. Because we maintain one of the largest in salon inventories of Russian hair in the UK, we can often carry out a consultation and a fitting on the same day. This allows you to walk in for a consultation and leave the salon with a full set of hair extensions if you wish.

Will you refit hair from another salon?

Yes, we can refit hair that has been fitted elsewhere. While we cannot guarantee the quality or lifespan of hair purchased from another salon, we are always happy to help improve your look and comfort. With our experience in precise placement and the use of higher quality rings, we can often achieve a noticeable improvement even when working with your existing extensions.

Why does Russian hair cost more than other types of hair?

Russian hair is highly sought after because of its natural softness, strength, and texture that blends seamlessly with Western hair. Unlike mass produced extensions made from Asian or Indian hair, which are often coated in silicone, Russian hair retains its natural quality and longevity. This means it can be reused many times and looks natural for far longer, making it a worthwhile investment even though the initial cost is higher.

Are longer hair extensions more expensive?

Yes. The price of hair extensions increases with length because longer hair is rarer and takes more time for the donor to grow. Longer extensions also require more volume for a natural look, which adds to the cost. We stock Russian hair in lengths up to 26 inches, with ultra long hair available by special order.

Can I get a price quote before booking?

Yes. We publish a full and detailed price list on this page so you can see costs for each method by length and volume. During your consultation, we confirm the exact price before you commit, so there are no surprises. If you prefer, we can also provide an estimate by phone or video consultation.

Are hair extensions a cost effective choice?

Yes. While the initial cost may seem high, Russian hair extensions are reusable time and again. With proper care, they can last for years, making them far more cost effective than cheaper alternatives that need replacing after a few months. For many clients, the ability to refit the same hair multiple times significantly reduces long term costs.
