What Are Hair Toppers?

Hair toppers, sometimes called wiglets, are lightweight hairpieces designed to cover specific areas of thinning or hair loss on the scalp. Unlike full wigs, they do not cover the entire head but blend seamlessly with your natural hair, adding volume and coverage exactly where it is needed. We offer Classic, Frontal and Halo toppers, each custom made from Russian hair, colour blended to match your natural shade and designed to give back confidence through discreet, natural coverage.

Toppers are best for women experiencing partial hair loss or thinning in specific areas of the scalp. A Classic Topper is ideal for general thinning at the crown or parting, a Frontal Topper is suited to recession at the front hairline or temples, and a Halo Topper is designed for more advanced thinning where there is not enough hair to support full clips. Because toppers are smaller than full wigs, they feel lightweight and are easy to wear daily, helping restore confidence by creating fuller, thicker hair.

The Classic Topper

The Classic Topper is the most familiar format, chosen for general thinning at the crown, the parting or the top of the head. The base is shaped around the exact area you want to cover, while the outer edge meets your own hair so that colour, length and movement continue naturally into your finished style.

It suits women who still have a strong frame of healthy hair at the sides and back, restoring the top rather than changing the whole silhouette. Small clips sit in the supporting hair around the coverage area, positioned from your measurements and checked at fitting for comfort and security through a full day.

Like every piece we make, a Classic Topper is made to measure from Russian hair over 8 to 12 weeks, with mono, silk or hybrid bases chosen around scalp appearance, airflow and weight. Made to measure Classic Toppers start from £1,050.

For the hairline and temples

The Frontal Topper

The Frontal Topper is designed for recession and thinning at the front hairline and temples, the pattern many women notice first at the mirror and the hardest to disguise with styling alone. The piece rebuilds the hairline itself, with fine hairs set into a soft edge that reads as skin where it meets the forehead.

Because the front of the head frames the face, realism here is everything. The hairline is mapped to your own, density graduates the way natural growth does and the parting can be worn where you have always worn it. The result photographs naturally and holds up at conversation distance.

Frontal Toppers suit recession from traction, hormonal thinning and frontal hairline loss, and are made to measure from Russian hair over 8 to 12 weeks. Your consultation confirms whether a frontal or classic design will blend most naturally with your own hair.

For advanced thinning, from £1,100

The Halo Topper

The Halo Topper answers more advanced thinning, where there is no longer enough strong hair on top to support the clips of a traditional topper. Instead of relying on the thinning area itself, the halo design draws its security from the stronger hair around the sides and back of the head.

That construction makes it the gentlest option for fragile hair. Nothing pulls on the very hair you are trying to protect, the weight is carried where your hair can comfortably hold it and the coverage on top remains full, soft and completely natural to the eye.

For many women the halo is the bridge between a topper and a wig, keeping their own hair in the picture for longer. Halo Toppers are made to measure from Russian hair over 8 to 12 weeks and start from £1,100.

What Makes Our Hair Toppers Different?

Unlike most wigs and toppers made from processed, silicone coated hair and mass produced in China, ours are custom made from Russian hair to ensure they look completely natural and are fully tailored to you. We are the only company in the UK to offer three different types of custom made toppers: Classic, Frontal and Halo. As there is no one size fits all solution, each topper is designed to provide coverage exactly where you need it while blending seamlessly with your own hair.

At every stage, from selecting the hair to choosing the base and the final fit, you are involved in creating a personalised piece rather than purchasing an off the shelf product. We colour match by selecting a few Russian ponytails, drawing hairs from them and recombining them into larger strands that are sewn into the topper, a multi tonal blend that matches your natural colour without dye.

We then guide you in choosing the most suitable base, a breathable mono base for comfort and airflow, a soft silk base in the parting where a natural scalp appearance matters most, or a hybrid base combining both, silk at the parting for realism and mono elsewhere for lightness. Once your design is confirmed, your custom topper is ready within 8 to 12 weeks.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is Tatiana Karelina the leading choice for hair toppers in the UK?

Tatiana Karelina hair toppers are handmade from Russian hair and individually tailored to your exact area of thinning for a natural and secure result. Each piece is blended strand by strand using multiple ponytails to match your colour, density and texture without dye. With Classic, Frontal and Halo toppers available, plus bespoke base options including hybrid bases, every piece is designed around the wearer. Lightweight, breathable construction ensures comfort, while in salon or video consultations and discreet UK wide delivery make the process personal and accessible.

When should I choose a hair topper?

Choose a topper if you have partial hair loss or thinning and want discreet coverage that blends naturally. Thinning on top with healthy hair at the sides and back is the classic pattern a topper answers.

What types of hair loss are toppers suitable for?

Our hair toppers suit women with partial hair loss or thinning in specific areas, diffuse thinning at the crown or parting, recession at the front hairline or temples including frontal fibrosing alopecia, or more advanced localised loss where a wider area of coverage is needed. Your consultation confirms the most appropriate design.

How do I choose between a Classic, Frontal and Halo topper?

A Classic Topper is best for general thinning at the crown, parting or top of the scalp. A Frontal Topper is designed for recession at the front hairline and temples. A Halo Topper suits more advanced thinning where there is not enough strong hair to support traditional clips. We assess your hair and recommend the option that gives the most natural and secure result.

How is the colour of my topper matched?

We select several Russian ponytails and draw strands from each to create a bespoke blend, recombined and sewn into the topper for a multi tonal result that matches your natural hair without dye. The finished piece blends seamlessly and looks far more realistic than a flat, single shade.

What types of topper bases are available?

Mono bases offer breathability and a natural scalp appearance, silk bases give the most realistic parting, and hybrid bases combine materials in one topper, silk at the parting for realism with mono elsewhere for lightness. We guide you to the right base for your coverage and comfort.

How long does a custom topper take to make?

Once your design is confirmed, your topper is usually ready within 8 to 12 weeks. A 50% deposit confirms the order and the balance is due on completion. The time goes into hair selection, colour blending and building the base by hand.

Will my topper look and feel natural?

Yes. Every topper blends with your own hair in both colour and texture. Russian hair and custom colour blending make the result soft, natural and individual to you, and the bases are chosen for comfort, so the piece feels light and secure for daily wear.

Will my topper stay in place?

Yes. When properly fitted, a topper feels secure and comfortable throughout the day. The attachment method and base are chosen according to your level of hair and area of coverage, so the piece remains stable while blending naturally.

How do I care for my hair topper?

Gently, much like natural hair. We recommend washing with suitable products, brushing carefully and storing it properly when not in use. We guide you on the exact routine for your piece so it stays beautiful for as long as possible.

What do hair toppers cost?

Hair toppers and wiglets start from £615, made to measure Classic Toppers from £1,050 and Halo Toppers from £1,100. Your exact quotation is confirmed at consultation, after the design, length, density and base are agreed.
