What Are Clip In Hair Extensions?

Clip in hair extensions are perfect for clients who want instant volume or length without any long term commitment. They are especially suited to those who prefer to wear their natural hair day to day and only add extensions occasionally, such as for special occasions, events, photoshoots, or nights out. Because they are lightweight, reusable, and easy to apply, they offer maximum flexibility and convenience, allowing you to transform your look effortlessly while protecting the health of your natural hair.

Our hand tied Russian hair clip ins blend seamlessly, offering a luxury option that is both reusable and versatile. Because they can be removed at any time, they are gentle on your natural hair and, with proper care, can last for years.

What Makes Our Clip In Hair Extensions Different?

Our clip in extensions are handmade from Russian hair and crafted with hand tied wefts, ensuring the thinnest, most comfortable fit. Frequently Asked Questions

Why are Tatiana Karelina clip in extensions considered the best in the UK?

Our clip ins are handmade from Russian hair and custom blended in salon to match your colour, density and texture. The all in one design sits flatter, feels lighter and is easier to apply than multi piece sets. Flexible bases are shaped to lie flush against the head for a natural finish and comfortable wear. With in person and video consultations and reusable Russian hair, Tatiana Karelina is the leading choice for premium clip in extensions.

When should I choose clip in extensions?

Choose clip ins if you want instant, commitment free length or volume that you can wear occasionally and reuse for years.

What makes your clip in hair extensions unique?

Our clip in extensions are handmade from Russian hair and crafted with hand tied wefts, ensuring the thinnest, most comfortable fit. By blending several shades into each weft, the result is multi tonal and natural without the need for dye. Each set comes in a reusable Matryoshka clutch purse, reflecting our heritage and commitment to sustainable beauty.

How many pieces are included in a set of clip in hair extensions?

We use an all in one system that combines five wefts into a single piece. This creates a seamless full head look with fewer clips, greater comfort, and faster application compared to sets with multiple pieces.

Why is an all in one clip in better than a set with multiple pieces?

An all in one piece avoids bumps from overlapping wefts and reduces the number of clips, making it lighter and more comfortable to wear. It also saves styling time while ensuring a smooth, secure, and natural finish.

How are clip in hair extensions installed?

Clip ins are designed for easy, at home application. Simply section your hair, snap the polymer coated clips into place, and blend. The clips are secure yet gentle, with a soft rubber bar that grips without pulling or damaging your natural hair.

How long will clip in hair extensions last?

With proper care, our Russian hair clip ins can last several years. As they are not worn daily, they avoid the wear and tear of permanent methods, making them one of the longest lasting extension options.

Do clip in hair extensions damage natural hair?

No. Because clip ins do not use glue, heat, or chemicals, they are the least damaging method. When applied and removed correctly, they cause no stress to your natural hair, making them safe for occasional or regular use.

Can clip in hair extensions be styled with heat tools?

Yes. Since our clip ins are made from Russian human hair, they can be curled, straightened, or waved just like your own hair. We recommend always using a heat protectant spray to maintain their longevity.

Do you offer curly or textured clip in hair extensions?

Yes. We stock Russian hair in a wide range of textures including straight, wavy, frizzy, and curly. This ensures your clip ins blend seamlessly with your natural hair type without the need for styling tricks.

How do I care for my clip in hair extensions?

Treat them like your natural hair. Wash occasionally with sulphate free shampoo, condition lightly, and store in the provided Matryoshka clutch or a satin bag. Avoid excessive heat and brush gently to maintain their condition.

How do I place an order?

For made to measure clip ins, we recommend visiting our London or Manchester salon, where you can select the hair type, thickness, colour, texture, and length. If visiting is not possible, we offer video consultations. Ready to buy sets are available online or by phone.
