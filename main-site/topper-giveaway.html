Every Month, One Woman Gets Her Confidence Back

Behind every application is a woman who misses her hair. Every month Tatiana Karelina chooses one and fits her with a custom topper, free of charge. Made for her, fitted in the salon, and shared to show others they are not alone.

What to expect

How It Works

  1. ApplyFill in the form and tell us about your hair loss in your own words. There is nothing to buy and no numbers to collect.
  2. Panel reviewEvery application is read in full by a small panel at Tatiana Karelina, including our senior topper specialists.
  3. The decisionThe recipient is chosen on her personal circumstances, the difference a topper would make to her daily life, and the story she shares. This is not a draw and nothing is left to chance; the panel's decision is final. The name is announced on this page on the last day of the month.
  4. Your fittingThe recipient is fitted at our London or Manchester salon. The visit is filmed so the story can be shared.

The small print, in plain words

Who Can Apply

You are experiencing hair loss, thinning or alopecia. This includes hair loss from cancer treatment and other medical conditions. You are a UK resident aged 18 or over and can travel to our London or Manchester salon, and you are happy to tell us about your circumstances in the application.

You agree to be filmed at your fitting. The footage may be shared on our website and social media, and filming consent is a condition of entry. One application per person per month, and previous recipients may apply again after 12 months.

Applications close at 23:59 on the 25th of each month. Anything received after that is automatically carried into the following month, so your story is never wasted.
