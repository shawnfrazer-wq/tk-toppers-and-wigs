- **Only 2 topper/wig before/afters on the main `/hair-extensions-before-after-photos/` page.** That page is overwhelmingly extension work (micro rings/bonds/tapes/wefts/clip-ins/ponytails/braids). The topper and wig before/afters live on `/clip-in-toppers-and-wiglets/` and `/medical-wigs/` — I fetched from both.
- **Homepage carousel images `3rd-on-Carouse_.webp` and `4th-on-carousel.webp` were kept as before/afters** because the alt text clearly identifies them as topper (Silvie) and wig (Tatiana) transformations, even though the filenames are generic. Flagging so you can confirm these are OK to place alongside the numbered before/afters.
- **`Copy-of-20.webp` (Heidi topper) and `Copy-of-22.webp` (Lori medical wig)** on the homepage duplicate the same numbered images from the toppers and wig pages — kept both under distinct filenames in case Shawn wants the specific homepage crops.
- **`Homepage-Carousel-Wigs.webp` alt is "Bespoke medical wig consultation for hair loss"** — a *consultation* photo, not a wig product shot. Good for a hero on `/wigs/medical-wigs` if a moody consultation image suits.
- **`Classic-Topper.webp` alt is just "hair toppers"; `Frontal-Topper-Caropusel-.webp` alt is just "toppers"** — the alts are lazy on the live site. Both are homepage carousel product shots suitable for classic/frontal page heroes or offer sections.
- **`Halo-Topper-Carousel-.webp` alt is descriptive** ("Halo hair topper matched to brunette hair in a salon, showing natural volume and coverage"). The best-labelled of the three.
- **`Our-Point-of-Difference.png` 404s** on the origin (referenced in the live HTML but the file has been deleted or moved). `tatiana-with-ponytails.webp` is a strong Russian-hair alternative that was successfully harvested.
- **Prices**: none of the caption text carries prices. WordPress alt text is a plain descriptive sentence, no £ values. The `price` column in the manifest is empty by design — that data is not on the page.
- **Hair-loss page has only ONE image** — the page hero (`Hero-Mobile-355x768-3-3.webp`). No topper/wig section images on that page for us to harvest; it renders more as an intake-form page than an editorial one.
