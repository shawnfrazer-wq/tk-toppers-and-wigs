# Tatiana Karelina Toppers and Wigs: Site Crawl and Content Map

Prepared to structure the new toppers and wigs site around the questions clients actually ask. Sources are the full crawl of tatianakarelina.co.uk, the live topper page FAQ block, and the existing blog.

## Crawl summary

Roughly 180 live URLs. Breakdown:

Service and location pages: home, clip-in-toppers-and-wiglets, medical-wigs, hair-loss, hair-loss-solutions, toppers-london, toppers-manchester, plus the extensions pages (micro rings, micro bonds, clip-ins, tapes, wefts, braids and ponytails) and their London and Manchester variants, prices, before and after photos, the two salon pages, book consultation, contact, press, policy, meet Tatiana, session work.

Blog: around 150 articles across categories hair-toppers, wiglets, hair-loss, knowledge, russian-hair, micro-rings, micro-bonds, tapes, tape-extensions, clip-ins, clip-in-ponytail, weft-hair-extensions, hair-extensions.

Two findings that matter for this build:

1. A hair loss subdomain already exists. hairloss.tatianakarelina.co.uk is live, titled Hair Loss Solutions, describing bespoke toppers and wigs, Russian hair, dye free colour matching, free consultation. We need to know if this is a live landing page, a test, or the start of this project before we pick the domain path, because it may already occupy the subdomain we discussed.

2. Location pages exist for several services (toppers-london, toppers-manchester and so on) but there is still no dedicated Classic, Frontal or Halo topper page, and no custom or human hair wig page beyond medical wigs. The three Discover buttons on the homepage carousel have nowhere deep to land.

## The gap in one line

Toppers and wigs are promoted with equal billing but under served on delivery: no dedicated page per type, thin specifics, thin imagery and video, and the FAQ depth is buried on a single page rather than spread across the pages people actually search for.

## New site page architecture (money pages)

Each page below should carry its own before and afters, its own video slot, its own FAQ block with FAQPage schema, and target its own primary term.

## Branding

The site replicates tatianakarelina.co.uk's design language exactly — fonts are the only permitted difference (Playfair Display + Inter here). White dominant, ivory used once per page at most as a single accent band, no bordered cards, all body copy fully justified with hyphens off at every breakpoint. Every design-review comparison shows the live main site alongside ours at the same scale. See AGENTS.md → Design system for the full rules.

The wordmark and roundel come from `/public/brand/tk-logo.png` (dark ink version taken from the main site). The hero on every money page carries the large TATIANA KARELINA wordmark in the top-centre calm space of the photograph, matching the main site's in-hero mark placement.

## Before and afters

The current gallery is a single carousel with pricing captions and strong descriptive alt text, for example a 20 inch mono based topper and a 26 inch fully laced wig. On the new site this becomes filterable by type, so a halo topper researcher sees halo results and a wig researcher sees wig results. Reuse the existing caption and alt text convention, which is already good for SEO.
