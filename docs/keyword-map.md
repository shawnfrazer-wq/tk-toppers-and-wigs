## Data honesty note

No Google Search Console, Google Keyword Planner, Ahrefs, Semrush or paid data source is connected to this project. Nothing in this document is a measured search-volume figure. Every keyword listed is a language-and-intent judgement derived from three real sources:

1. The 12-month revenue mix you provided (Toppers £156,880, Clip-Ins £104,634; Toppers averaging ~10 units/month at ~£1,297).
2. The 180-URL crawl and full FAQ set from tatianakarelina.co.uk in docs/content-map.md.
3. Public competitor phrasing on Vixen and Blush, The Extensionist, Inanch London, Ambitious Lengths and the general hair-loss commercial market.

Where I write "primary intent" or "buyer state" I am reasoning from that base. When you connect Keyword Planner or Ahrefs, we replace inferred priority with measured volume in one pass. Until then, treat this map as a coverage plan, not a volume-ranked plan.

## How to read each block

For every money page:

- URL: the built route.
- Buyer state: what a person searching this term is trying to do (informational, considering, comparing, buying, or already-decided local).
- Primary keyword: the single term the title, H1 and URL should be optimised for.
- Secondary keywords: 3 to 6 supporting queries the page must clearly answer inside the copy.
- Question queries: long-tail questions that become FAQ items or subheadings on the page. These are the queries AI answer engines are most likely to cite.
- Notes: any positioning call worth being explicit about.
